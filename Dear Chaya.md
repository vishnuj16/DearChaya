# Dear Chaya,

*Dear Diary,* 

My name is Kiran. I am 10 years old. I study at Beacon Academy School in Chennai. I like my school a lot. So do my parents. Or I think they do. They must. After all, they send me there every day. And I have heard Appa say that he would love to go to school again.

I made a mistake at school today. A bad one. It’s why I am writing here.

My class teacher, Mrs Meena, told me to tell my parents, or at least tell someone about this. But I do not want to tell my parents. They will scold me. And I don’t want to tell my friends. They are angry at me already. We already fought today. So actually, I don’t want to tell anyone at all.

But somehow, and I don't know how, Amma found out. She has been looking at me very doubtfully ever since I came home from school. She hasn’t talked to me about it yet, though. But I know she will. After dinner. Right before sleeping. Appa gets into bed late because of his work. And Amma always uses that time to advise me a lot before sleeping. I think she is going to do something like that today.

Which is why I am writing here. This was my Appa’s old diary. His office gave it to him as a gift a long time ago. Must have been three years ago, I think. I was still a child back then.

But when I saw the diary on the shelf today, I remembered the diary entry classes our English teacher, Kavitha Miss, taught us. She told us how people write down their feelings and everything in a diary when they want to. When they need to. And right now, this feels like the perfect thing to do.

Rohit gave his diary a name in his English exam when we had to do a diary entry. Me and my friends asked Kavitha Miss if that was wrong and if she would cut marks for him. He already had a very good mark. I think it was around 33 out of 40\. Anyway, Kavitha Miss said that it was completely fine to give your diary a name. So I think I should give you a name too. 

I don’t know what, though. I will get to it. I have wasted ten minutes thinking of names already. I need to write.

So, I had a lot of friends in school. I had Ganesh, Rohit, Monisha, Arjun, Sagar, Diya, and Gloria. I could keep going. I like to say that all my classmates are my friends. When actually, the seven people I said are actually my only good friends. We sit together all the time. The others in the class are just normal friends.

One other name I would have chosen if I was writing last year was Chaya.  
She is no longer a good friend.  
You see, our school rearranged our classes this year, and Chaya had to go to B Section. When I saw that she made new friends there, I didn’t feel like talking to her much. We were her friends first, weren’t we? So why is she talking to us so little now? At first, she used to come find us during lunch, during snack break, and after school. But lately, she comes less and less. She is always with Manju and Harini. Always. First, she stopped coming after lunch. Then after snacks. And today, she didn’t even come after school. Rohit and Diya were angry too. The others didn’t care, but us three did. And that’s the problem.

But I don't understand why it's ok, though. Why do the others think it's okay for Chaya to abandon us? When we had so much fun? Our group isn't the same anymore. We don't have fun like before anymore.

During PT period, whenever we played kho-kho or kabaddi or badminton, she was always the second-best player among us. I was—no, I *am*—the best.

I am the best player in our group. But a lot of people disagree with me. They think she is better. Chaya says so as well. I don’t understand how, though. Sure, she might be a little faster at running, but I am fast too, and I am definitely quicker at badminton. She only wins games by accident because I usually get stuck playing with Sagar or Diya, who aren't very good, while she gets paired with Monisha or Ganesh, who are way better. That’s the only reason she wins.

I will not argue that she isn't the best at maths, though. Even though I am not bad at it, I always get marks between 30 and 35, whereas she gets 35+. And I would get much lower than that if the two of us didn't study together before exams. Ever since she went to B Section, she hasn't studied with me anymore. She says we can’t, even though she claims she wants to, because we are in different classes with different teachers, so our question papers might be different. I told her the exam papers are the same for all sections. Because they are. But she just said B Section learns things differently, and studying together would be too difficult.

Whenever we had a free period, we used to play hand cricket, or sometimes when Arjun and Monisha weren't in the mood for it (they are bad at hand cricket), we played book cricket.   
Chaya is very bad at both. The worst. We all celebrated when we were paired to play against her. But unlike Arjun and Monisha, she doesn't argue or cry when she loses. She laughs. I don't laugh when I lose, though. Chaya sometimes turned to me and said that it's a very small and irrelevant game. Why not have fun and laugh when you lose?  
Stupid idiot. What could be more important than hand cricket? Or beating our friends at the game?

She likes taking pictures with me. When we went on a field trip to a park, we were allowed to bring a phone, and we took a lot of pictures together.   
“The others just aren’t photogenic. They don’t look good in photos. Unlike us," she said.   
I had asked her immediately what being photogenic meant. Apparently, it's when someone always looks good in photos. And I did. I knew that. My mother and grandmother also always said the same. They always tell me I am the most handsome boy. Chaya didn't say all that, though. She just said I was photogenic.

She is not very funny. But she often thinks she is. She always makes those bad jokes, trying to make the group laugh, but it always fails. Nobody laughs. Everyone actually laughs at her for trying to make a joke. They say she is cringey and has bad humour. I laugh along with them. But I don't agree with them. I always thought her jokes were quite funny. And I can tell that sometimes she is hurt when we laugh at her. I stop laughing when I see that.

So, I don't know why the others are so cool with her leaving us. Why don't they hate her for it? Everyone just says to let her go and be friends with whoever she wants. Except Rohit and Diya, who for some reason had started insulting her every chance they got. They say they are angry at the abandonment and call her all sorts of names. I don't want to write down what they call her. Amma might find the book and scold me for knowing those words. 

And so, we fought today. I asked Chaya during lunch why she doesn't talk to us anymore, and she said that she had new friends now and doesn't have the time to hang around with us. Everyone left when she said that, except me. And when we were alone, Chaya told me quietly not to ask this again because some of my friends don't really like her. Then she left too.

When I got back to my friends, I saw Rohit and Diya fighting with the others. I wanted to fight the others alongside them for not caring about Chaya, but I hated Rohit and Diya more. They did not like her at all. It was obvious. I knew why I was angry at Chaya, but I didn't know why they were. And so I asked them. And for that, Rohit laughed meanly and said, “Shut up, you buffoon, don't say this stuff to anyone. You don't hate her, you miss her.”

I hated the way they were laughing at me and Chaya. So I hit him. I punched Rohit on the nose. And I stood there and smiled before I felt bad about it. 

When it happened, some random teacher came in and split us apart before Rohit could get up and hit back. We were all taken to our classroom and scolded badly. Mrs Meena scolded me a lot and asked me why I hit Rohit. I didn't want to tell. So, I said very little. Only that Rohit insulted me. Mrs Meena tried a lot but gave up in the end but with a warning that if anything like this happens again, then my parents would be called. She also suggested talking to someone if I didn't want to talk to her.

I don’t know why, though. I don't know how this is supposed to help. Sure, you now know what happened. Am I supposed to feel better? Because I still hate Chaya. I am still sad that I had to sit alone without friends in school for the first time today. And for the first time, I don't look forward to going to school tomorrow morning. I have no friends now. 

Unless I count you, of course. Dear diary.   
Oh wait. Now I understand why Meena Miss had told me to talk to someone. She wanted me to go and make new friends. Or, I think that’s what it means. Probably. And if so, you are the start of it. Also, I can't promise to write every day; Amma might find you, so I have to hide you. And she might call for dinner anytime now as well. See you tomorrow.

And lastly, I think I have the perfect name to give you now as well. I will call you Chaya from now on.
